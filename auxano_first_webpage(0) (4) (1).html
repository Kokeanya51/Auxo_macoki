🌿 About MACOKI

Welcome to MACOKI

Patient-Centred Healthcare

MACOKI is a patient-centred healthcare organisation committed to making quality healthcare more accessible, convenient and compassionate.


Our Approach

MACOKI is designed around a simple principle: healthcare should not only treat illness — it should also prevent disease, promote health and protect communities.


Our approach combines clinical healthcare, maternity services, public health, community health, diagnostics, pharmacy, nursing, midwifery, preventive healthcare, rehabilitation and appropriate specialist referral.


Patient-Centred Care

We aim to create an environment where individuals and families can receive professional healthcare guidance throughout different stages of life — from consultation to diagnosis, treatment, prevention, follow-up and health education.

His leadership vision is to develop MACOKI into a trusted healthcare organisation where clinical services and public health work together to improve individual and community wellbeing.

🎯 Mission & Vision

What Drives Us

🎯 Our Mission

To provide accessible, affordable, safe, compassionate and high-quality healthcare through a multidisciplinary approach that integrates medical care, maternity services, nursing, midwifery, public health, community health, diagnostics, pharmacy, preventive healthcare and appropriate referral.

👁️ Our Vision

To become a trusted and respected healthcare institution in Nigeria, recognized for excellence in patient care, maternity services, public health, community health, diagnostics, preventive healthcare and professional service delivery.
